Our Mission

Our mission as Mzizi Catering is to serve our potential clients quality food and taste. We want clients to experience affordable, comfort food made from scratch with love — traditional food that warms the soul and brings families together.

Our Vision

Our vision is to set the standards for luxury and culinary innovation through traditional meals. We aim to be the leading traditional catering brand in South Africa by 2030.

What Makes Us Unique

Mzizi Catering delivers authentic traditional food AND modern fusion dishes. We are 100% black-owned, women-led, and community-focused. Our ingredients are sourced from local farmers.

Our History

Mzizi Catering was established in 2022. Two cousins, Lerato Dlamini and Thabo Nkosi, saw an opportunity after experiencing poor service from other caterers. They opened a business that would cater to African people with traditional food that is quality, hygienic, and mouth-watering.

"We wanted to bring back the taste of home — the food our grandmothers made with love."
— Lerato Dlamini & Thabo Nkosi, Founders
